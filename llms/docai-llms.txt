# doc.ai

> doc.ai, Inc. was a privacy-first enterprise artificial intelligence platform for
> healthcare, founded by Sam De Brouwer and Walter De Brouwer. It ran decentralized
> "data trials" that let individuals train models on their own genomic, exposomic,
> wellness and hematology data, and issued the $NRN (Neuron) token in a sale that
> closed in October 2017 — the reason it is carried in Pantera Capital's portfolio
> under the crypto sector. Sharecare, Inc. completed its acquisition of doc.ai on
> 2021-02-23, absorbing the Unwinding Anxiety, Serenity, VERIFIED and Passport
> products. The doc.ai brand is retired: the apex domain no longer resolves to a
> website, and no doc.ai developer portal, API, or specification exists.
